    Appendix D — Glossary¶

    Acronyms and technical terms used in the book, each with a short definition, the chapter where it is introduced, and its Spanish equivalent. Hovering over an acronym anywhere in the book shows its expansion.

    Spanish terms and their sources¶

    Every Spanish term was looked up in Spanish-language sources rather than translated. The tag after each term names the source where it was found:

    Tag Source
    GRA Grainger & Stevenson, Análisis de sistemas de potencia (McGraw-Hill, Latin American translation)
    WEE Weedy, Sistemas eléctricos de gran potencia (Reverté, Spain)
    FRA Fraile Mora, Máquinas eléctricas (Spanish original, Spain)
    CHA Chapman, Máquinas eléctricas (McGraw-Hill, Latin American translation)
    ALE / HAY Alexander & Sadiku, Fundamentos de circuitos eléctricos; Hayt et al., Análisis de circuitos en ingeniería
    BUR / STR / CHP Burden & Faires, Análisis numérico; Strang, Álgebra lineal y sus aplicaciones; Chapra & Canale, Métodos numéricos para ingenieros
    TAH / HIL Taha, Investigación de operaciones; Hillier & Lieberman, Introducción a la investigación de operaciones
    SOGL Regulation (EU) 2017/1485, official Spanish text: directriz sobre la gestión de la red de transporte de electricidad
    CACM Regulation (EU) 2015/1222, official Spanish text: directriz sobre la asignación de capacidad y la gestión de las congestiones
    REE Red Eléctrica: online glossary and operating procedures (procedimientos de operación)
    FIUBA / UNLP Course programs of Universidad de Buenos Aires and Universidad Nacional de La Plata
    WIKI Spanish Wikipedia (weaker source, used only when the others give nothing)
    † No Spanish source found: suggested translation; in practice the English acronym is usually kept

    Two regional conventions show up across the sources. Books from Spain (WEE, FRA) and the EU and REE texts use tensión, nudo, falta and relé; the Latin American translations (GRA, CHA) use voltaje, barra, falla and relevador. Argentine course programs mix them (tensión with barra and falla). Both are listed where they differ.

    Terms¶

    Power systems and circuits¶

    Term Meaning Spanish Ch.
    Active power Real power \(P\), the average power delivered, in W or MW potencia activa [SOGL, FRA]; potencia real [GRA, CHA] 9
    Admittance / impedance \(Y = G + jB\) and \(Z = R + jX = 1/Y\) admitancia / impedancia [all textbooks] 9
    Apparent power \(\lvert S \rvert = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2}\), in VA or MVA potencia aparente [FRA, CHA, ALE] 9
    Bad data Measurements with gross errors that state estimation must detect datos erróneos [GRA] 22
    Bay Group of switchgear connecting one line, transformer or load to the busbars posición [REE] 13
    Branch In ToOp, any line or transformer between two nodes rama [GRA, partial] 14
    Breaker-and-a-half Substation layout with three breakers for every two circuits interruptor y medio [REE] 13
    Bus admittance matrix \(Y_{\text{bus}}\), relating nodal current injections to nodal voltages matriz de admitancias de nudos [WEE]; matriz de admitancias de barra [GRA] 14
    Bus-branch model Grid represented as electrical nodes and branches, without switches modelo barra-rama † 13
    Busbar Conductor in a substation to which circuits connect barra (barras colectoras) [SOGL, REE]; embarrado [WIKI] 13
    Busbar coupler Breaker that connects two busbars of the same substation acoplamiento de barras [REE]; interruptor acoplador [SOGL] 13
    Busbar outage Loss of a whole busbar and every element connected to it; a multi-outage in ToOp indisponibilidad de barra † 18
    Busbar reassignment Moving a line or injection from one busbar to another within a substation cambio de barra (reasignación) † 13
    Busbar splitting Opening couplers so a substation runs as two electrical nodes separación de barras [REE] 13
    Capacity calculation Computing cross-zonal transmission capacity for the market cálculo de capacidad [CACM] 21
    Cascading failure Sequence of trips where each outage overloads further elements fallo en cascada (ES) / falla en cascada (LatAm) † 20
    Circuit breaker Switch able to interrupt load and fault currents interruptor [SOGL, REE, GRA]; disyuntor [WEE] 13
    Complex power \(S = V I^* = P + jQ\) potencia compleja [GRA, FRA, WEE] 9
    Congestion State in which a network element would exceed its limit congestión [CACM, REE] 12
    Congestion management Measures that keep flows within limits (redispatch, topology changes, countertrading) gestión de las congestiones [CACM] 12
    Contingency Possible outage of one or more elements considered in security analysis contingencia [CACM, SOGL, GRA] 19
    Contingency analysis Computing the grid state after each contingency in a list análisis de contingencias [SOGL, GRA] 17
    Countertrading Cross-zonal exchange initiated by TSOs to relieve congestion intercambio compensatorio [SOGL, CACM] 12
    Critical branch In ToOp, a branch above its limit; counted by the critical branch count metric elemento crítico de la red [CACM] 19
    Cross-coupler flow Power that would flow through an open busbar coupler if it were closed; ToOp's KCL imbalance at busbar A flujo a través del acoplamiento † 18
    Disconnector Switch that isolates equipment but cannot interrupt load current seccionador [REE, WEE, FRA] 13
    Distance protection Relay that trips when the apparent impedance falls inside a zone of the R-X plane protección de distancia [WIKI]; relé de distancia [WEE] 20
    Distributed slack Power imbalance shared among several generators instead of a single slack bus nudo de compensación distribuido † 15
    Double busbar Substation layout with two busbars and a coupler doble barra [REE] 13
    Double limits In ToOp, operational bands (90–100 % of rating) used by the *_limited metrics límites dobles † 19
    Fast decoupled load flow Power flow method using constant, decoupled P–θ and Q–V matrices flujo de potencia desacoplado rápido [GRA, partial] 15
    Fault level Short-circuit power at a node potencia de cortocircuito [FRA]; nivel de cortocircuito [WEE] 20
    Flat start Power flow initialization with all voltages at 1 p.u. and 0° inicio plano [GRA] 15
    Flow-based market coupling Market clearing that respects grid constraints expressed with zonal PTDFs acoplamiento de mercados basado en flujos [CACM, REE, composite] 21
    Injection In ToOp, any generator, load, battery or converter station connected to a node inyección † 16
    Island / islanding Part of the grid disconnected from the rest after an outage isla; funcionamiento en isla [REE] 17
    Line switching Opening or closing a line to change power flows maniobra de líneas [FRA, partial] 28
    Line-to-line voltage Voltage between two phases, \(\sqrt{3}\) times the phase voltage tensión de línea (tensión compuesta) [FRA, WEE]; voltaje línea a línea [GRA] 10
    Load-break switch Switch that can interrupt load current but not fault current interruptor-seccionador † 13
    Node-breaker model Grid represented with physical nodes, busbar sections and switches modelo nodo-interruptor † 13
    Off-nominal turns ratio Transformer ratio different from the ratio of the base voltages relación de transformación no nominal [GRA, adapted] 11
    Outage Element out of service, forced or planned indisponibilidad [SOGL]; salida (de línea) [GRA]; descargo (planned) [REE] 17
    Overload Flow above an element's admissible limit sobrecarga [SOGL, REE] 19
    Overload energy ToOp's main metric: sum over branches of flow above the limit, worst contingency per branch energía de sobrecarga † 19
    Per-unit system Normalizing quantities by base values so transformers disappear from equations sistema por unidad [CHA]; valores por unidad [FRA] 10
    Phasor Complex number representing the amplitude and phase of a sinusoid fasor [all textbooks] 9
    Power factor \(\cos \varphi = P / \lvert S \rvert\) factor de potencia [all textbooks] 9
    Reactive power \(Q\), power oscillating between source and fields, in var potencia reactiva [SOGL, all textbooks] 9
    Redispatch Changing generator outputs to relieve congestion; the costly alternative to topology changes redespacho [SOGL, REE] 12
    Relay Device that detects abnormal conditions and commands breakers relé [WEE, FRA, REE]; relevador [GRA] 20
    Relevant substation In ToOp, a substation eligible for splitting under the preprocessing rules subestación relevante † 22
    Remedial action Action that restores or preserves operational security; preventive (before) or curative (after a contingency) medida correctora [CACM]; acción preventiva / acción correctiva poscontingencia [REE] 12
    Short-circuit current Current flowing during a fault corriente de cortocircuito [SOGL, GRA, WEE, FRA] 20
    Slack bus Reference bus whose injection balances the power flow and whose angle is zero nudo de referencia (flotante) [WEE]; barra de compensación [GRA] 15
    Susceptance / reactance Imaginary parts of admittance and impedance; DC power flow uses \(b = 1/x\) susceptancia / reactancia [all textbooks] 9
    Switching distance In ToOp, the number of switch operations needed to realize a topology (Hamming distance) distancia de maniobra † 13
    Symmetrical components Decomposition of unbalanced three-phase quantities into positive, negative and zero sequence componentes simétricas [GRA, WEE, FRA, FIUBA] 10
    Synchro-check Verification that voltage angle and magnitude differences are small before closing a breaker verificación de sincronismo † 20
    Tap Winding connection point that changes a transformer's ratio or phase shift toma [SOGL, FRA]; derivación (tap) [GRA, CHA] 11
    Thermal limit Maximum current an element can carry without overheating límite térmico [CACM]; capacidad térmica [SOGL, REE] 19
    Three-winding transformer Transformer with three windings, modeled as three branches around a star node transformador de tres devanados [GRA]; de tres arrollamientos [WEE] 11
    Tie line Line connecting two control areas or TSO grids línea de interconexión [SOGL]; línea de enlace [WEE] 14
    Topological remedial action Remedial action that changes the grid topology (switching, busbar splitting) medida correctora topológica; cambios topológicos [SOGL, REE] 0
    Topology Connectivity of the grid given by switch states; in ToOp, a set of station actions, disconnections and PST setpoints topología de la red [SOGL, CACM] 0
    Topo-vect Grid2Op's vector giving the busbar (1 or 2) of every element in each substation topo-vect (term kept) † 13
    Transmission capacity Maximum power that can be transferred between areas capacidad de transporte [WEE]; capacidad de intercambio [CACM] 21
    Voltage stability Ability to keep acceptable voltages after disturbances estabilidad de tensión [SOGL, WEE, FIUBA] 20

    Term Meaning Spanish Ch.

    Condition number \(\kappa(A) = \lVert A \rVert \lVert A^{-1} \rVert\), the sensitivity of a solution to perturbations número de condición [BUR, CHP, STR] 4

    Incidence matrix Branch-by-node matrix with +1 and −1 at each branch's end nodes matriz de incidencia [STR, GRA] 2

    Sparse matrix Matrix with mostly zero entries, stored compactly matriz dispersa [BUR, CHP]; matriz rala [STR] 4

    Term Meaning Spanish Ch.
    Branch and bound Exact method for integer programs that explores and prunes a tree of subproblems ramificación y acotamiento [TAH, HIL]; ramificación y poda [WIKI] 23
    Constraint Condition a feasible solution must satisfy restricción [HIL, TAH]; condición de vínculo [FIUBA] 23
    Crossover Variation operator combining two parents cruce [TAH, HIL] 24
    Decision variable Quantity chosen by the optimizer variable de decisión [HIL, TAH] 23
    Descriptor Feature that places a solution in a MAP-Elites cell; in ToOp, e.g. split substations and switching distance descriptor de comportamiento † 25
    Dominance (Pareto) Solution a dominates b if it is no worse in every objective and better in at least one dominancia de Pareto [WIKI] 27
    Duality Pairing of an optimization problem with a dual whose optimum bounds it dualidad; problema dual [HIL, TAH, FIUBA] 23
    Elitism Keeping the best solutions unchanged into the next generation elitismo [WIKI] 24
    Epsilon-constraint method Optimizing one objective while bounding the others método de la ε-restricción † 27
    Feasible region Set of all solutions satisfying the constraints región factible [HIL, STR]; espacio factible [TAH] 23
    Fitness Score used for selection; ToOp's DC fitness is a negated weighted sum of metrics función de aptitud [TAH] 24
    Heuristic / metaheuristic Rule of thumb / general strategy for guiding approximate search heurística / metaheurística [TAH, HIL, FIUBA] 24
    Hyperparameter Setting of an algorithm chosen before running it hiperparámetro [WIKI] 26
    Hypervolume indicator Volume of objective space dominated by a front, bounded by a reference point indicador de hipervolumen † 27
    Mutation Variation operator that randomly changes a solution mutación [TAH, HIL] 24
    Non-dominated sorting Ranking a population into successive Pareto fronts ordenamiento no dominado † 27
    Objective function Function the optimizer minimizes or maximizes función objetivo [HIL, TAH, CHP] 23
    Pareto front Set of objective vectors of non-dominated solutions frente de Pareto [WIKI] 27
    Repertoire Archive of elite solutions indexed by descriptor cells in MAP-Elites repertorio † 25
    Reproducibility Obtaining the same results when an experiment is repeated reproducibilidad [WIKI] 26
    Shadow price Change of the optimal objective per unit relaxation of a constraint precio sombra [HIL]; precio dual [TAH]; valor marginal [FIUBA] 23
    Simulated annealing Local search that accepts worse moves with a decreasing probability recocido simulado [TAH]; templado simulado [HIL] 24
    Surrogate model Cheap approximation of an expensive evaluation modelo sustituto [WIKI] 29
    Weighted-sum scalarization Combining objectives into one by a weighted sum; misses unsupported Pareto points escalarización por suma ponderada †; método de pesos [TAH] 27
