    Appendix B — Bibliography¶

    Every textbook recommended in the book, with the chapters that use it, how many surveyed university syllabi cite it (Appendix A), its best access route on archive.org, and its Spanish edition. Free online texts that have no print counterpart in this list (course notes, documentation, papers) are cited directly in the chapters.

    Access labels are defined in the conventions. Access status was checked against the archive.org metadata and loans APIs in September 2026; Spanish editions were checked against library catalogs (Open Library, university catalogs) and publisher sites.

    Summary of what the checks found:

    • Borrowable in English: most classic undergraduate texts have at least one borrowable edition, often an older one.
    • Spanish editions on archive.org: none is in the lending library or the print-disabled collection. The Spanish copies that exist there are user uploads; they are listed as USER-UPLOAD (ES) only for books with no FREE or BORROW copy in any language.
    • Spanish translations: most classic undergraduate textbooks have one; graduate and research texts (Wood & Wollenberg, Kundur, Boyd & Vandenberghe, Deb, Eiben & Smith, and others) generally do not.
    • "Syllabus-cited" in the Spanish column means the Spanish edition appears in a surveyed syllabus but was not confirmed in a catalog.
